Flow 1: Morning 60-Second Cash Triage & Priority Action

Pattern source: flow-app-shell + flow-ai-chat · Entry point: App Launch / Browser Bookmark

Steps

  1. 13-Second Hero Glance (Safe Runway & Net Headroom)

    Owner opens the app. The screen renders the green/amber Executive Status Banner ("🟢 YOUR CASH POSITION IS SAFE: 100% Payroll Funded") alongside 4 vital non-overlapping money tiles: Safe to Spend from Bank (₹37.39L), Cash in Till (₹4.20L), Expected Market Dues (₹2.22L), and 30-Day Upcoming Commitments (₹10.96L).

    Primary action: Review #1 Priority Action Card → Step 2

    If Cash is Tight (Bank + Till < Commitments) → Hero displays amber deficit banner with exact shortfall date and suggested expense deferral advice.
  2. 2One-Click Human-in-the-Loop Action (WhatsApp Takada)

    Directly adjacent to the cash health banner, the owner sees today's highest-leverage operational task: "📞 Collect ₹29,295 from Om Supermarket, Bavdhan (Open 45 days · 3 salesman slips awaiting deposit)".

    Primary action: Tap '📲 Send WhatsApp Reminder' → Opens WhatsApp Web / Mobile Draft

    Human-in-the-Loop Gate (flow-ai-chat rule 6): Pre-fills polite vernacular text with invoice reference and UPI handle. Never sends automatically; owner retains full control to edit or send.
  3. 3Conversational Copilot Inquiry (Voice/Chip)

    Owner taps a pre-populated chip ("Can I pay salaries on 1 Oct?") or types in English/Hindi. The system instantly returns the exact grounded calculation from the verifiable engine, accompanied by plain vernacular explanation and AI provider badge.

    Primary action: Tap Dismiss (✕) or tap follow-up question → Returns to cockpit

Flow 2: Monday Receivables & Chronic Khata Recovery

Pattern source: flow-tables + flow-errors · Entry point: 'Receivables' Tab

Steps

  1. 1Filter Chronic Retailers by Aging Tier

    User selects 'Receivables' tab. Toolbar presents quick filter chips: "🔥 45+ Days Overdue (19 Chronic)", "All Customers", and "⚠️ Over 30 Months (Check with CA)". Active count and total outstanding rupees update immediately.

    Primary action: Select aging filter → Table updates in place

  2. 2Salesman Collection Slip Protection (Anti-Double-Call Guard)

    Before calling a retailer, the system checks unbanked salesman collection slips. If Salesman Ramesh collected ₹15,000 yesterday (awaiting physical bank deposit), a prominent badge reads: "🛡️ Salesman Ramesh collected on 29 Sep; not banked yet. Do not call."

    Primary action: Skip retailer or review collection memo → Protects customer relationship

    If debt > 30 months → Displays Limitation Act Section 18 alert advising owner to secure written acknowledgement from customer.
  3. 3One-Click Polite WhatsApp Reminder

    Owner clicks "📲 WhatsApp". Pre-composed message is generated in polite business language with UPI payment handle.

    Primary action: Review in WhatsApp Web / App & Send → Returns to receivables queue

Flow 3: 60-Second Evening Till Close & Bank Deposit Matching

Pattern source: flow-forms + flow-tables · Entry point: 'Cash Book' Tab

Steps

  1. 1Role Access & PIN Protection

    If role is Owner, cash book opens instantly. If Counter Staff, physical drawer notes and khata balances are locked behind Owner PIN (default "0000"). Staff enters PIN to unlock.

    Primary action: Enter Owner PIN → Unlocks Cash Book

    If Accountant role and owner unchecks sharing → Accountant sees formal bank records only, protecting private informal cash memos.
  2. 260-Second Day-Close Balancer

    Owner or cashier enters: Opening Cash, Today's Takings, Cash Expenses (Hamali/Petty), Banked Cash, and Counted Physical Cash at close. System verifies drift against ±₹50 tolerance threshold and tags till as 'Balanced' or 'Drift to check'.

    Primary action: Record in Cash Book → Updates on-device local ledger

  3. 3Salesman Cash Deposit Linking (Decision 55)

    Salesmen submit individual shop collection slips. When banked into the current account, the engine links the single consolidated cash deposit to the 3 collection slips and presents it as a verified SUGGEST match in the review queue.

    Primary action: Review suggested match → Marks invoices paid
